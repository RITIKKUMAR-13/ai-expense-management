# Spendwise AI — Financial Studio Direction

## Chosen Design Direction: Financial Studio

Spendwise AI moves away from the previous command-desk treatment into a calmer **Financial Studio** design. It uses a warm paper-like light workspace for planning and review, with a deep slate dark theme that remains equally legible rather than merely inverting colors. The product should feel like a carefully prepared monthly money workbook with decisive digital tools.

## Core Principles

1. **Budget clarity first:** Every category budget visibly shows planned amount, amount spent, and amount remaining.
2. **Theme parity:** Light and dark modes are complete design states, with a reliable control and a dedicated current-preference key.
3. **Cleaner hierarchy:** Short labels, generous space, and focused panels make the money overview easier to scan.
4. **Distinctive details:** Subtle paper grain, category-color bands, compact money columns, and rounded planning cards make the product recognizable without visual noise.

## Color and Material

Light mode uses warm ivory, ink navy, muted mist-blue, and a precise jade accent. Dark mode uses blue-black slate, graphite panels, soft silver text, and a cooler jade accent. Coral, violet, and amber remain category signals only; they are never used as broad background color.

## Dashboard Structure

The overview starts with a calm monthly summary, followed by spending rhythm and a compact insight module. A dedicated budget ledger makes category comparison explicit through three columns: **budget**, **spent**, and **left**. This structure is reused in the Budget Studio so users never need to infer remaining amounts from a percentage bar alone.

## Brand Essence

**Spendwise AI is a composed personal money studio where every category has a clear amount left to use.**

Personality adjectives: **calm, analytical, approachable**.
